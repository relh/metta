## **Software Engineer \- Contractor**

**Remote (Global)**

### **About Us**

Softmax develops multi-agent reinforcement learning simulations focused on social learning scenarios. Our work explores how multiple agents can maintain individual identity while achieving coherence as a unified system—similar to multicellularity in nature.

### **The Role**

We're seeking a remote contract software engineer to build high-quality systems and tools. You'll work independently on diverse engineering challenges that support our research and product development, focusing on scalability, performance, and clean architecture. This role is ideal for self-directed engineers who thrive with autonomy and take ownership of their work.

### **Key Responsibilities**

- Design and implement robust, scalable software systems
- Build tools and infrastructure to support research and development workflows
- Optimize system performance
- Collaborate asynchronously with team members to translate requirements into technical solutions
- Write clean, maintainable code with appropriate testing and documentation
- Manage your own schedule and deliverables with minimal oversight

### **Requirements**

- Strong software engineering background with demonstrated experience building complex systems independently
- Ability to work autonomously and manage projects from conception to delivery
- Strong written communication skills for remote collaboration
- Self-motivated with strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail
- Comfortable working across time zones and managing asynchronous workflows

### **Nice to Have**

- Experience with machine learning, data science, or AI systems
- Familiarity with distributed computing
- Background in data visualization or analytics tools
- Previous remote contracting experience

### **What We Offer**

- Competitive hourly compensation (starting at $60/hr)
- Flexible schedule and complete location independence
- Opportunity to work on cutting-edge research problems
- Collaborative yet autonomous work environment
- Direct impact on innovative multi-agent systems

### **Company Culture**

Our team values coherence, both in our technical work and our approach to collaboration. We appreciate engineers who are thoughtful about systems—both technical and social—and who can work effectively with minimal ego and maximum impact. Many team members have backgrounds in practices that cultivate insight (meditation, contemplative practices, etc.), though this is not a requirement.

### **Work Arrangement**

This is a remote hourly contractor position open to talented engineers worldwide. We prioritize results and quality over hours worked, seeking individuals who can deliver exceptional work independently.
