This note explains the idea behind Recyclopedia.

## What Recyclopedia is

Recyclopedia is not a polished publication track. It is a living notebook where we put concepts while they are still evolving.

The entries are intentionally lightweight:

- short hypotheses
- design sketches
- failed approaches
- revised definitions after implementation pressure

## Why we use it

Some ideas become clearer only after repeated cycles of building, debugging, and reframing. Recyclopedia captures those cycles so we do not lose the trail of reasoning.

Instead of pretending each note is final, we keep a searchable history of how ideas change.

## What to expect

Expect rough edges, temporary terminology, and links between notes that move over time. The goal is useful continuity, not perfect polish.

If a concept survives enough pressure, we later promote it into a fuller essay or external write-up.
